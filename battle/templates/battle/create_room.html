The basics

  • The host creates a room and shares the code. Friends join with it.
  • Pick Alpha or Bravo in the lobby. Up to 5 players per team, plus spectators.
  • The host picks the mode and starts. Once it starts, nobody new can play. Late arrivals can only watch.
  • Everyone except the host taps Ready before the host can start. The host can also pick which course the questions come from.
  • Questions come from your department, at levels below your own. In a mixed room, the lowest level decides.
  • If a whole team disconnects for 45 seconds, the other team wins.
  • Every player on the winning team earns a win. Winners get up to 200 XP (less after 2 wins today or rematching the same opponents). Everyone else gets 10.
