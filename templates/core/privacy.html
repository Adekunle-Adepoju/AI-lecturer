Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Rovea is a study platform for university students. This page explains what information we collect, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

When you sign up or sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google profile basics. You may also give us your department, level and chosen courses. As you use Rovea we record your study activity: lessons and quizzes you complete, answers you submit, practice test results, XP and leaderboard position, timetable entries, battle-mode matches, focus sprints, and the messages you send to the AI lecturer.

We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, time spent) to understand how the platform is used.

How we use it

To run your account, personalise your lessons and timetable, score quizzes and battles, show leaderboards, keep the service secure, and improve Rovea. We do not sell your personal data.

Who else sees or processes it

Other students: your display name and XP may appear on leaderboards and in battle rooms.

Google: handles sign-in, and its Gemini AI service processes the messages and content used to generate lessons, quizzes and chat replies.

Infrastructure providers: your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and the application runs on Render.

We may share aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics with partners. We do not share your name or email with them.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and protect forms, and your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To have your account and data deleted, or to see, correct or export what we hold about you, email us at the address below.
